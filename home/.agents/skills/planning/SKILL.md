---
name: planning
description: Guidelines for planning, investigation, and problem solving. Use this skill when researching an approach, investigating a bug, debugging unexpected behavior, designing a solution, evaluating options, or forming a hypothesis about how something works.
---

When planning or investigating:

- Avoid assumptions at all costs. Vet everything you propose or consider
  against actual implementation research.
- If a code path leads into a seperate service or repository, please ask for access
  if that would help to complete your understanding.
- Consider that your hypothesis may be wrong. Challenge your own thinking and
  use alternate perspectives to gut-check proposals.
- If a behavior or desired outcome is unclear, stop and ask for clarification. It is always
  better to clarify with questions rather than making assumptions.
